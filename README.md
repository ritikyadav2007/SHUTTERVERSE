## ✦ Historical Record Details & Archival Standards

Every historical image and sacred artwork includes:
1. **Title**
2. **Historical Period / Dynasty**
3. **Country or Region**
4. **Estimated Date**
5. **Artist, Sthapati, or Atelier**
6. **Medium & Fabrication Technique**
7. **Curatorial Description & Theological Context**
8. **Original Museum Source** (National Museum New Delhi, Archaeological Survey of India, Met Museum, British Museum, Louvre, Cairo Museum)
9. **Copyright & License Information** (Public Domain / Open Access / Cultural Heritage Record)
10. **Classification**: Clearly distinguished as *Authentic Ancient Sculpture*, *Imperial Miniature*, *Cave Bas-Relief*, *Historical Photograph*, or *Contemporary Photographic Masterwork*. Synthetic/AI recreations are never mislabeled as original historical records.

## ✦ Viewer & Interaction Capabilities

- **Cinematic Charcoal Fullscreen Lightbox**: Dark background (`#0E0E0E`) with zoom toggle (`Z` key), previous/next image navigation (`←`/`→`), and optical EXIF / historical provenance HUD.
- **Comprehensive Detail Drawer**: Full curatorial dossier with historical source, copyright status, and technical telemetry.
- **Live Search**: Search by historical period (e.g. *Chola*, *Gupta*, *Mughal*), country/region (*India*, *Egypt*, *Greece*), subject (*Shiva*, *Krishna*, *Gandhi*), medium, or camera.
- **Curated Salon (Favorites)**: Save items to a persistent personal collection (`localStorage`) with a live header badge and drawer.
